This is a guide, not a rulebook. Every colour in your palette can be worn anywhere: clothing, makeup, nail polish, jewellery, eyewear, ties, hats, scarves and beyond. Use this as a reference to understand what works with your natural colouring. Colour analysis is about empowerment, not restriction.

The Light Spring palette contains thousands of colours. The swatches here are carefully chosen examples of what to look for: neutral undertone, medium to medium-high intensity, light value. Your colours sit closest to neutral, inching warm. Use these as a visual compass, not a checklist.

Screen tip: For the most accurate colour reference, turn off your blue light filter and set your screen brightness to around 80 percent or higher. On iPhone look for Night Shift under Settings > Display & Brightness. On Samsung look for Eye Comfort Shield under Settings > Display.

Online shopping tip: When unsure about a colour, try asking an AI assistant: "Is [Brand] [Product] in [Colour Name] cool, warm, or neutral toned? Does it suit Light Spring?" Keep in mind AI pulls from across the internet and isn't always accurate, since sources can conflict. Always check samples in store when possible.

Scarves, Hats & Wraps
Near-face accessories matter most
Anything worn close to your face has the same impact as a top or blouse. A powder blue scarf, a rose beige hat, or a lilac wrap near your face will all be flattering. Tip: a scarf in one of your brighter blues near the face can lift an otherwise neutral outfit.

Lips
Your most flattering lip colours are neutral to warm, medium to medium-high pigmented, light to medium value. Neutral pink, coral, warm pink, and peach all sit in your sweet spot.
Everyday: neutral pink and peach
ILIA Balmy Tint Hydrating Lip Balm, Runaway
RMS Beauty Lip2Cheek, Smile
Merit Signature Lip, Antibes
Look for products described as neutral pink, warm pink, or soft peach. These are your most wearable everyday options, colour without ever looking done up. Tip: any of these worn lightly give a beautiful your-lips-but-better result.
Polished: warm coral and light red
e.l.f. O Face Satin Lipstick, Feeling Myself
Charlotte Tilbury K.I.S.S.I.N.G, Dancefloor Princess
Merit Signature Lip, Cabo
Look for neutral pink, warm coral, or a lighter warm red products. These are your go-to for work, evenings out, and any occasion where you want your lip to say a little more. Note: satin and matte formulas here run a little more pigmented than a balm, so a lighter hand goes a long way.
Evening: warm red
Charlotte Tilbury K.I.S.S.I.N.G, Stoned Rose
Merit Signature Lip, Vermillion
Look for a neutral or warm red with a lightweight, buildable finish rather than a fully opaque matte. These build gradually rather than committing to full intensity in one swipe, so you can keep it light or build it up depending on the occasion. Tip: pat rather than swipe to keep the intensity in your medium range.

Foundation & Tinted Moisturiser
Foundation rule: neutral to warm undertone.
What to look for
This applies across every skin tone and depth. A neutral match works beautifully, and a slight lean toward warm can also work well. Avoid anything described as strongly pink or ashy. Tip: swatch on your jawline in natural light. A match that blends seamlessly is right; one that pulls noticeably pink or grey is off.
Eyes
Your eyes do best with airy, natural tints rather than heavy pigment: understated warmth over bold colour blocking. A soft eye works beautifully with a stronger lip, and a stronger eye pairs well with a quieter lip.
Eyeshadow: what works
Base eyeshadow: ivory white, warm rose, warm taupe, soft lilac, understated peachy tints. Depth: cocoa, sweet plum, navy, pine bloom. Accents: periwinkle, turquoise, medium teal, rosehip red. Avoid: heavily cool silver metallics, and anything fully matte-black.
Eyeliner
Warm brown, ocean blue, or navy are your most versatile liners, each giving a different depth. Tip: a softly smudged brown liner with a neutral pink lip creates an effortless, natural finish.
Mascara
Light brown or light navy mascara are your best choices. A very cool-toned, fully opaque black can feel harsh against your lighter colouring.
Brows & Beard
Brow & beard guidance
Match your brow product to your natural hair colour or go one shade darker. Look for neutral or warm taupe depending on your depth. Avoid anything strongly ashy or cool-grey. Tip: ask for "neutral" or "warm-neutral" when shopping brow pencils or powders.
Hair Colour
Your most beautiful hair colour is often the one you were born with. Warmth in this placement can show up differently for different people: it might come through more in a beard than the rest of the hair, or read more or less warm depending on the light, so treat this as a general direction rather than a fixed rule.
Your best hair colours
Your naturals: blonde through light brown, kept neutral to warm.
What to ask your colourist
Ask for "neutral-warm toned," "not too ashy," or show them your palette directly. Tip: when in doubt, go lighter and warmer rather than darker and cooler.
